Konnect Packaging International LLP

Konnect Packaging International LLP manufactures VCI rust-prevention packaging, food-grade packaging and specialty industrial packaging for export, storage, transport and industrial supply chains. The company is based in Nagpur, India with European operations and serves customers looking for corrosion protection, barrier packaging, moisture control and sustainable packaging materials.

VCI Packaging Solutions

VCI product lines include VCI Kraft Paper, VCI PE Laminated Paper, VCI 3-Ply Paper, VCI LDPE Film, VCI Strength Fabric, VCI MET PET Laminated Paper, VCI 4-Ply Fabric, VCI Shrink Film, VCI Desiccant, VCI Masterbatch, VCI Power Stretch Film, Industrial Wax Paper, Alu Barrier Bags and VCI Eco Paper.

Food & Agro Packaging

Food and agro packaging products include SMP Bags, Bulk Tea Packaging Bags, PE-Coated Paper, Wax Coated Paper, Paper Aluminum Pouches, Standing Pouches, HDPE Laminated Paper Bags, Sugar Paper and Multiwall Paper Bags.
